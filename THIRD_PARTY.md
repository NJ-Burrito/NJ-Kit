# Third-party credits

## Fonts

No font files are included. `pdf-typography/scripts/fetch-fonts.mjs` downloads Cairo, Aref Ruqaa and JetBrains Mono (SIL OFL 1.1) from their upstream sources on your machine. Every font type is git-ignored.
